# 🛒 Discount Impact Analysis – Eniac Case Study

This project analyzes the business impact of discount strategies for **Eniac**, an e-commerce technology company.  
It was developed as part of the **Data Analytics program at WBS Coding School** and later refined for portfolio presentation.

The analysis addresses a real stakeholder conflict between marketing and investors regarding the long-term effects of discounting.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A debate existed within the company:

- **Marketing Team** argued that discounts increase customer acquisition, satisfaction, and retention.
- **Board Investors** were concerned that discounts increase order volume but reduce overall revenue and harm premium brand positioning.

**Goal:**  
Use data analysis to evaluate whether discount strategies are beneficial from a business perspective.

---

## 🧠 Analytical Approach

The project follows a structured, business-driven data analysis workflow:

1. Data cleaning and preprocessing of internal sales data  
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
3. Comparison of discounted vs. non-discounted products  
4. Revenue, quantity, and customer behavior analysis  
5. Insight generation and business recommendations  

The focus is on **interpretability and decision support**, not predictive modeling.

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

- Order volume vs. discount levels  
- Revenue impact of discounted products  
- Customer purchase behavior under discounts  
- Temporal patterns (seasonality, trends)  

Visualizations were used extensively to communicate insights to non-technical stakeholders.

---

## 📈 Key Insights (Summary)

- Discounts significantly increased order volume, especially from new customers.
- Average revenue per order decreased as discount levels increased.
- Customer retention effects were mixed: some customers returned, others only purchased during promotions.

These findings highlight the trade-off between **short-term growth** and **long-term revenue sustainability**.
